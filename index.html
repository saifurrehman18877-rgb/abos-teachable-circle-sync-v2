Features

No more manual cross-referencing

Paste your Teachable and Circle exports, and let the tool find students who haven't logged in and aren't already in your 'needs-encouragement' space.

Set your own inactivity threshold

Decide how many days of inactivity should trigger a nudge (e.g., 14 days). The tool will only flag students who haven't logged in within that window.

Ready-to-reimport CSV

Get a CSV with just the emails of students who need encouragement, formatted exactly for Circle's import tool. No extra steps.

How it works

1. Paste or upload your Teachable and Circle exports

Copy and paste your CSV exports from Teachable and Circle, or upload them directly. The tool handles the rest.

2. Set your inactivity threshold

Choose how many days of inactivity should trigger a nudge (e.g., 14 days). The tool will only flag students who haven't logged in within that window.

3. Download your ready-to-reimport CSV

Get a CSV with just the emails of students who need encouragement, formatted exactly for Circle's import tool. No extra steps.

FAQ

Does this tool work with other platforms besides Teachable and Circle?

No, this tool is specifically designed to work with Teachable and Circle. We're exploring integrations with other platforms in the future.

How often should I run this tool?

We recommend running this tool at least once a week, or whenever you notice a spike in student churn.

Can I automate this tool?

No, this tool is static and does not support automation. However, you can set up a recurring task to run the tool manually.
